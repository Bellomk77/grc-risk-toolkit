Executive summary

  • 6 of 10 risk scenarios remain high or critical after existing controls. The largest is ransomware delivered by phishing encrypts core systems and backups (residual score 12.6 of 25).
  • Control maturity is lowest in Recover (0% implemented). Missing governance controls (risk appetite and tolerance are defined and communicated; supplier risks are assessed, prioritized and monitored) leave risks without clear ownership.
  • Implementing the top 5 roadmap items (3 small, 2 medium effort) cuts total residual risk by 21% and reduces high or critical risks from 6 to 1.
  • Risk appetite: Low for member data and availability of core banking; moderate for internal tooling.
